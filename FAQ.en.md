# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — BRAN Org

### 1. What is BRAN Org?
BRAN Org is an independent initiative that rescues, standardizes, and provides public Brazilian scientific data (such as conference proceedings and regional journals) via free APIs and open datasets (`JSON`/`CSV`).

### 2. Can I use the datasets in my research?
Yes! All datasets are open under the **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** license for academic and non-commercial research use.

### 3. Can the data be used to train commercial AI models?
No. The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license explicitly restricts scraping or ingesting datasets for training commercial Artificial Intelligence models without prior authorization.

### 4. How can I suggest a new event or journal for BRAN Org to map?
You can open an **Issue** in our repositories or fill out our [Data Suggestion Form](https://forms.gle/bran-org-contato).
